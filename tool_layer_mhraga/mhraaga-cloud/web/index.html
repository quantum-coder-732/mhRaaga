Rules of the raga

Every song follows aroha and avaroha, vādī and samvādī a fourth or fifth apart, the pakaḍ, and ends with a tihai landing exactly on sam.

Speak and listen

Dictate your idea or your lyrics, and hear the words recited over the music, in Hindi, Marathi, Sanskrit or English.

Tuned to 732 · 932 · 936

Choose Sa at 183, 233 or 234 Hz, the three sacred numbers folded down two octaves.
