About voices

Samples are stored privately for you. This site plays them back and keeps them ready; it does not clone voices itself. To make a cloned voice, export the samples and use a local tool such as VoiceStudio on your own machine (AGPL-licensed, so keep it separate from this service), and only for voices you have consent to use.
